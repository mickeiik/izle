izle is a TV player for someone who only knows classic television. One grid of big show cards, and the OK button plays the next episode. You run everything else from your phone.

For the person you set it up for

It works like a TV channel, not a streaming app

Episodes follow one another on their own. When something goes wrong, the TV shows a short message in large letters and goes back to the shows by itself. There is nothing to get lost in.

The app is in English and Turkish, with audio and subtitle languages chosen for each TV.

For you

Everything else, from your phone

izle companion is the Android app for you, the owner. It connects from the TV: hold OK, choose Settings → Connect a phone, and scan the code it shows.

  • Choose which shows are on each TV's home screen, in sections, in what order, and where to continue
  • See what is playing and pause, seek, skip or stop it from anywhere
  • Send a short message to the screen: “Dinner is ready!”
  • Browse your catalog, see what is new in it, and play a show on the TV
  • See the problems a TV ran into, and check every episode's sources

No Android phone? The server's admin page does all of it in any browser, on a phone or a computer.

Listen along

Your phone plays another version of the episode on the TV, a dub in another language for example, in sync with the picture, and keeps playing with the screen off. When two releases don't line up, tap once when you see a moment and once when you hear it.

Apps on Home

A card on Home can open another app on the TV. If you like, izle can be the TV's home screen, so switching it on opens izle. Optional: a TV whose own home screen insists on the Home button, as Google TV does, keeps it.

Your server, your catalog

izle ships no content. The server reads a catalog you host: a folder of JSON and posters that points at your HLS, DASH or MP4 videos, and live channels (HLS, DASH or MPEG-TS). A sample catalog of openly licensed films is included.

Small and private

One dependency-free Node.js package or a Docker image. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Stream URLs and tokens are kept out of logs and never reach the TV.

Try it in about 10 minutes

You need a computer with Docker, an Android TV, Google TV or the Android TV emulator on the same network, and an Android phone for the companion app.

  1. Build and start the server from a clone of the repository docker build -t izle-server . docker run -d -p 8787:8787 -v izle-data:/var/lib/izle izle-server
  2. Install the TV app from the latest release, with adb.
  3. Pair the TV by approving its 6-digit code on the admin page.
  4. Add a few sample films to its home screen and press OK.
  5. Connect your phone: install izle companion from the release, then on the TV open Settings → Connect a phone and scan the code.
